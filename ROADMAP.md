# Roadmap

Provena is an experimental retrieval library. These are **areas to investigate**, not promises or scheduled releases.

## Near-term focus

- Improve external developer onboarding and examples.
- Keep packaged public exports, documentation and release artifacts consistent.
- Evaluate retrieval quality with broader, independently reviewed queries and corpora.
- Characterize real-world memory and latency beyond the current benchmark fixtures.

## Ideas under consideration

Document-level result grouping, alternative ranking strategies, diagnostics, and larger-corpus acceleration may be explored when consumer use cases justify them. They are not part of the current public API.

Answer verification remains the responsibility of the consuming application. See the [API contract](docs/api.md) and [architecture](docs/architecture.md) for current behavior. Proposals with concrete examples or benchmarks are welcome in the project's issue tracker.
